# Product

## Register
product

## What Vizora is
Vizora is a local-first, universal visualisation platform. Tagline: **See beyond information.** People turn structured text and data into flowcharts, mind maps, concept maps, timelines, charts and dashboards, edit them by hand, and take them away as images, PDFs, data files or static embeds.

## Users
Designers, analysts, product managers, developers, business professionals, students and independent creators. They have notes, outlines or a spreadsheet and need something clear to show other people, without learning a heavy tool or signing up for one.

## Purpose
Make information understandable at a glance, quickly, with the person staying in control. Everything generated stays fully editable.

## Principles
- **Local-first and private.** Projects live in the browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded; Vizora keeps no other copy. Backups are explicit downloads, and the product never implies cloud backup or sync.
- **Honest.** Capabilities are labelled available, in development or planned. Marketing never claims what the app cannot do. Generation is rule-based, not AI, and says what it could not read. No AI service or key is required.
- **Never lose work.** Autosave with a visible status; conflicts between tabs are surfaced, never silently overwritten; imports are validated and previewed first and never overwrite; destructive actions are confirmed.
- **Editable and portable.** A versioned, renderer-independent document format; lossless JSON, CSV, PNG, SVG, PDF and static HTML embeds.
- **Accessible.** Keyboard-complete (an Inspector does everything the pointer can), WCAG-contrast tested, reduced-motion respected.

## Anti-references
Hype and AI-washing; dark patterns; feature claims ahead of the build; cluttered chrome that competes with the canvas.

## Scope today (through F4)
Foundation and marketing site; diagram engine (flowchart, mind map, concept map, timeline); data engine (CSV, TSV, JSON, Excel and pasted import; 13 chart types; dashboards); workspace and project management (dashboard, search, sort, reorder, move, backup and restore, storage panel, collapsible panels, full-screen canvas); static embeds.

## Not built
AI-assisted generation, accounts and sync, live or hosted interactive embeds, collaboration, `.xls` import, PDF for single charts and diagrams, a table renderer.

## Plans
A Free edition is complete and has no trial clock. Paid tiers are described as planned where unbuilt; entitlements are checked centrally.
